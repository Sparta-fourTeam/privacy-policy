doodledoodle Privacy Policy

Operator: TeamDoodle
Last reviewed: To be determined

This document is a pre-release draft. It will be finalized after verification of the game's actual data processing practices.

TeamDoodle ("we", "us", or "the operator") is committed to protecting the privacy of users of doodledoodle ("the Game").

This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information processed in connection with the Game, their purposes, retention, third-party services, and user rights.

1. Information Processed and Purposes

Category Information Purpose
Guest Account Server-generated user identifier Account creation and identification
Google Account Linking Google account identifier (sub) Account linking and recovery
Gameplay Data Stage progress, in-game currency, upgrades, battle and reward records Saving and restoring game progress
Crash Diagnostics Crash logs, app and device information (subject to SDK configuration) Error analysis and stability
Gameplay Analytics Gameplay events, analytics identifiers, device information (subject to SDK configuration) Improving gameplay and user experience

Linking a Google account is optional. Users may play the Game with a guest account.

We do not directly collect or store Google account passwords.

The specific authentication tokens and server access records processed will be confirmed before release.

2. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for the period necessary to fulfill its processing purpose, subject to applicable laws.

When a user requests account deletion, the account and associated gameplay data will be processed for deletion.

Certain records may be retained where required by applicable law.

Specific retention and deletion periods for server logs, backups, and external analytics services will be finalized before release.

3. Third-Party Services

We plan to use the following services for diagnostics and gameplay analytics.

Service Provider Purpose
Firebase Crashlytics Google Crash reporting and diagnostics
GameAnalytics GameAnalytics Gameplay analytics

Actual integration, data categories, service provider relationships, and retention and transfer conditions will be verified before release.

4. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed outside the user's country when third-party analytics or diagnostic services are used.

Transfer recipients, destination countries, categories of data, transfer methods, timing, purposes, retention periods, legal grounds, and applicable user choices will be confirmed and disclosed as required before release.

5. Data Sharing and Processing

We manage disclosures to third parties and processing by service providers in accordance with applicable laws.

Actual data sharing and processing arrangements, including infrastructure providers, will be verified and described in the finalized policy.

6. Data Deletion

Personal information will be deleted when its purpose has been fulfilled or its retention period has expired.

Electronic data will be deleted using methods designed to prevent unauthorized restoration or recovery.

Information subject to legal retention obligations may be stored separately for the applicable period.

7. User Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of their personal information.

Requests may be submitted using the contact email below. Additional verification may be required to protect the account owner.

8. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their game accounts.

Deletion includes the guest account, linked Google account identifier, in-game currency, stage progress, upgrades, and related gameplay records.

We aim to protect personal information through appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls, authentication security, and encrypted communication.
