끄적끄적 디펜스 (DoodleDoodle) Privacy Policy

Operator: TeamDoodle · Representative and privacy contact: 안은석 (Eunseok Ahn)
Email: teamdoodle.dev@gmail.com

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains personal information processed during the 끄적끄적 디펜스 (DoodleDoodle) closed test and your rights. The game is currently free and does not offer real-money in-app purchases.

1. Information and purposes

  • Accounts and login: game user identifiers, nicknames, authentication information and, when linked, Google account identifiers are used to create, authenticate, link and manage accounts. We do not directly collect Google passwords. If you choose Google sign-in or account linking, the app sends the Google-issued ID token to our server for authentication. The token may contain your email address and name in addition to your Google account identifier. After validating the token, our server uses the account identifier and does not store the email address or name as separate account-profile fields.
  • Game progress: stage progress, currency, upgrades, battle and reward records are used to provide the game and save progress. Information stored only on the device and information sent to a server depend on the features enabled in the build.
  • Gameplay analytics: GameAnalytics processes analytics identifiers, device, app and session information, battle starts and ends, outcomes, waves reached, play time, kills, skill damage and card choices to help improve gameplay and balance. GameAnalytics infers country from the connection IP address.
  • Diagnostics: enabled Unity diagnostics features process installation identifiers, device, app, engine and session information, and crash, error and performance information for troubleshooting and stability.
  • Support and deletion requests: the sender's email address, nickname, account-linking information supplied by the requester and message contents are used to handle requests and verify ownership.

2. External services

GameAnalytics processes gameplay analytics under the operator's instructions. Real-money purchase information is not part of this game's data submission.

The game uses Unity Additional Recommended Data to process engine, session, crash, performance, device and installation-identifying information. Country is based on device locale. Individual in-game interactions are not included by default; developer-supplied custom metadata can differ.

Unity acts as a processor for technical support and business recommendations, and as an independent controller for customer-facing aggregated insights and modeling. Identifiable source data can be processed to produce those aggregated results.

3. Retention and deletion

We retain the game account and progress to provide the game and delete them when account deletion is completed. There is no recovery grace period. Signing up again with the same Google account creates a new user ID with initial progress.

The default retention period for personal information in Unity Diagnostics is 90 days. This applies to the Diagnostics service. For Additional Recommended Data used by Unity as an independent controller, retention is based on the processing purpose, the service relationship, legal retention obligations and the need to protect or defend legal rights. There is no blanket 90-day deletion promise for all such information.

GameAnalytics' current published Data Processing Addendum lists one month for raw and annotated data, one year for Player Warehouse, and 18 months for Player Lookups used to calculate retention. These are category-specific schedules, not a promise that deleting a game account immediately removes every external-service record. Related requests can be sent to our contact email.

Support information is used for as long as necessary to handle the request. If retention is legally required, only necessary information is retained under the applicable requirement and period. Electronic personal information is deleted using methods intended to prevent recovery.

4. International transfers

The external diagnostic and analytics services process the information described above outside your country. GameAnalytics' current published addendum identifies storage in AWS us-east-1 and processing providers in the United States. Information is transmitted through the SDK during use of the game for the purposes and retention periods described above.

  • Unity Technologies S.F. (United States), privacy contact: DPO@unity3d.com. Unity processes information in the United States and other countries where it or its service providers operate. The diagnostic and Additional Recommended Data described above is transmitted over the network while the game runs or is used. Purposes and retention criteria are described in sections 2 and 3. See section 9 of Unity's player privacy policy for its international-processing practices.
  • GameAnalytics ApS and its affiliates, privacy contact: privacy@gameanalytics.com. Its published addendum identifies processing of analytics information in the United States. Information is transmitted by the SDK during gameplay for the purposes and retention periods in sections 1 and 3.
  • Amazon Web Services EC2 and RDS in the Seoul, South Korea region provide hosting for the game server and database, including accounts and server-stored game information. This server and database processing takes place in South Korea. Account deletion and retention are described in section 3.
  • We use Google's Gmail to receive support requests, including your email address and message contents. See Google's privacy policy for its international-processing and security practices.

Contact our email address if you object to international or external-service processing or want to request restriction. You may also request account deletion. The current app does not provide a separate user switch for diagnostic or analytics transmissions. Stopping use of the app avoids new transmissions caused by subsequent app use; deletion of previously transmitted information can be requested separately.

5. Account deletion and your rights

Open the lobby menu, select Delete Account, enter your nickname as instructed, and confirm. You can also request deletion by email without accessing the app. See Account Deletion for details.

Send access, correction, deletion, restriction or consent-withdrawal requests to teamdoodle.dev@gmail.com. Ownership verification may be necessary to protect other users' accounts. We will explain the outcome or any restriction on fulfilling the request.

6. Protection and safe use

We restrict access to personal information to what is necessary and apply measures to protect personal and authentication information. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, device guest IDs or other login secrets.

7. Changes and references

Changes and their effective date will be announced on this page or in the game.
